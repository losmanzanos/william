Services

Who Will works with

01

Adolescents & parentsIndividual, family & parent coaching

For teens, therapy is a private place to be heard without judgment. School, friendships, identity, and family all bring pressure, and not all of it is easy to talk about with the adults in your life. Together we work on understanding what you're feeling and building the skills to handle it.

Parents get coaching to make sense of what their teen is going through, talk in a way that keeps the door open, and support more independence without losing the connection.

02

Young adultsIndividual therapy

Young adulthood is full of changes nobody really prepares you for. Feeling anxious, stuck, or unsure where you're headed is common. It's also a stretch of figuring yourself out that's worth taking seriously.

We look under the patterns holding you back and build habits that look more like the person you want to be.

03

Substance use groupComing soonYoung adults 18+, weekly, in person

A small weekly group for anyone at any stage of substance use recovery. It pairs ACT with peer support, built on one idea Will comes back to often: substances usually aren't the problem. They're the solution someone found. Understanding what they're solving makes room for real change.

About

Hi, I'm Will.

I grew up in the social media era, so I know the world teens and young adults are living in right now. That lets me take my clients' experiences seriously without excusing the patterns that aren't helping them. Holding both is a big part of the work.

I started in mental health in 2018, working in substance use recovery while I finished my psychology degree. After a year as a social worker for the state, supporting people with high-risk mental health needs, I earned my Master of Social Work at the University of Denver. I've worked with adolescents, young adults, and parents since 2023.

Trust comes first. Nothing meaningful happens in therapy until you feel comfortable with me, so that's where we start. Then we figure out what you want, and what's standing in the way.

My approach is mainly psychoanalytic, and I'm also trained in DBT, CBT, and ACT. I've trained extensively in substance use and work with people at every stage, from active use to long-term recovery. New to therapy or not, our sessions follow what you need, and I know that can change week to week.

FAQ

The practical stuff

Where are you located?+

1325 S. Colorado Blvd, Suite B108, Denver 80222, right off Colorado Blvd and I-25.

What does it cost?+

Individual sessions are $150 and family sessions are $175. The substance use group is $300 a month. HSA and FSA cards are welcome.

Do you take insurance?+

No. Denver Forward Therapy doesn't accept insurance or Medicaid.

Can sessions be virtual?+

Sessions are in person by default. Virtual works when getting to the office isn't possible, like transportation trouble or being sick, and for clients who leave for school.

How do I get started?+

Book a free 15-minute call. If you decide to move forward, your intake session is free if you decide within 48 hours that you don't want to continue. No pressure either way.
