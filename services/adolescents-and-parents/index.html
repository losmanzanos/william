Services

Adolescents & parents

Individual therapy for teens, family sessions, and coaching for parents.

For teens, therapy is a private space to be heard without judgment. Adolescence brings pressure from school, friendships, identity, and family, and it is not always easy to talk about with the adults in your life. Together we work on understanding what you are feeling and building skills to handle it.

For parents, I offer coaching to help you make sense of what your teen is going through, communicate in ways that keep the door open, and support their growing independence while staying connected.

How Will works with teens

Acknowledging that the last place most teenagers want to be is his office after an 8-hour school day, Will designs his work with teens around finding value in every session that creates incentive to keep coming back. Sessions remain confidential, but Will incorporates coaching calls and family sessions into his work to accommodate any issues that arise at home. Finding a middle path for everyone to allow growth and alliance is Will’s primary goal.
