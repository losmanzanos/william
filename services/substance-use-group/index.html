Substance use group

For young adults 18+. Weekly, in person.

A small, in-person weekly group for young adults (18+) at any stage of their substance use recovery.

Will uses a disease model to design an effective group program that combines ACT therapy with peer support. Will acknowledges that substances aren’t our problem; they’re our solution. Understanding what they’re solving beneath the surface makes room for empathy, growth, and sustainability. Wherever you are, you won’t be doing this alone.
