Services

Young adults

Individual therapy.

Young adulthood is full of changes we aren’t always prepared for, and it’s common to feel anxious, stuck, or unsure of your direction. Despite the struggle, this stage of self-exploration holds real beauty.

Together, we’ll look beneath the patterns holding you back and make room to build habits that reflect who you want to be, moving toward a life built around what matters most to you.

Sessions always begin in-person. Will accommodates virtual sessions for existing clients who are relocating for college, etc.
