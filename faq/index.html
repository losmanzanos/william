FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Where are you located?

1325 S. Colorado Blvd, Suite B108, Denver 80222. Right off of Colorado and I-25.

Get directions

What are your fees?

$150 for individual sessions, $175 for family sessions. We accept HSA and FSA. Group is charged at a monthly rate of $300.

Do you take insurance?

We do not accept insurance and Medicaid.

Are sessions virtual?

Sessions are primarily in-person, but Will accommodates virtual clients in the event of transportation issues, illness, or clients who begin attending school out of state.

How do I get started?

Book a free 15-minute call through the contact page or call (720) 734-4380.

To take some of the pressure off, your intake session is free if you decide within 48 hours that you don’t want to continue with therapy.
