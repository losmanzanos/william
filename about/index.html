About

Hi, I’m Will.

My passion for working with teens and young adults comes from my empathy for the world they are growing up in today. Having grown up in the social media era, I can validate my clients’ experiences without excusing unhelpful patterns, and I find that balance essential to helping them move in the right direction.

My career in mental health began in 2018. I got my start as a clinician in substance use recovery while earning my degree in psychology. After spending a year as a social worker for the state, supporting people with high-risk mental health needs, I attended the University of Denver and earned my Master of Social Work.

I have worked as a therapist with adolescents, young adults, and parents since 2023. Building trust is always my first priority, because I know nothing meaningful can happen in therapy until my clients feel safe with me. As we get to know one another, we come to a shared understanding of what they want to accomplish and, more importantly, what may be standing in the way. I am flexible, patient, and guided by intuition, and I adapt my work to each person I see. I look forward to every session and feel grateful to do the work I love every day.

I take an integrative approach that draws from several styles of therapy. I am primarily a psychoanalytic therapist and am also trained in DBT, CBT, and ACT. I have trained extensively in substance use as well, and I work with people at every stage, from active use to long-term recovery. Whether you have been in therapy before and are looking for a specific style, or you are new and not sure what to expect, our sessions will always be shaped around your needs, which I know may change from week to week.

The first step is always the hardest. To take some of the pressure off, your intake session is free if you decide within 48 hours that you don’t want to continue with therapy. If you or a loved one are ready to take the next step, it would be my honor to take it with you.
